# Why did it fail?

Click a run in the Runs view, or the CI item in the status bar, to see:

- **Why it failed**: the lines around the error of each failed job, with links to the files they mention.
- **Timeline**: every job and step on one time axis, so slow steps and queue time stand out.
- **Actions**: re-run failed jobs, cancel, approve deployments, download artifacts, **Fix in Chat**.

Logs open in the editor on the failing step, with errors marked and steps folded.
